# Audio environment assets v1

These five 20-second files derive from the CC0 recordings listed below.
Attribution is retained for transparent provenance, even though CC0 does not
require it. See `manifest.json` for complete source and final SHA-256 hashes,
source intervals, relative mix gains, filters, transformation notes, and format.

All sounds use [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
as declared by their author/uploader. The unmodified legal text is included in
`CC0-1.0.txt`. CC0 does not waive unrelated privacy, publicity or trademark rights.

Freesound inputs are publicly served high-quality MP3 previews; the cafe input is
the original Wikimedia Ogg upload. No account, payment, broadcast extraction,
voice cloning or speech synthesis was used. The output is intentionally labelled
by source and processing rather than claiming every preset is a field recording.

Each bed is downmixed/resampled, filtered, layered where stated, soft-limited,
wrap-crossfaded over 500 ms, faded to exact zero over 2 ms at the endpoints and
normalized to a peak of approximately -12.1 dBFS. All output files are mono
24 kHz signed PCM16 RIFF/WAVE with 480,000 frames (20 seconds).

## Verification boundary

File hashes, format, duration, levels, clipping and exact-zero loop endpoints
were checked programmatically. Direct audio audition was unavailable to the
preparing agent; no listening-quality or source-content audition is claimed.
Audition the final beds for intelligibility, distracting source content, and
perceived loop quality before accepting them for production use.
